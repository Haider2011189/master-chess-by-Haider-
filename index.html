How to Play Chess

  • Objective: Checkmate your opponent's King so it cannot escape capture.
  • Pawns: Move forward 1 square (or 2 squares on their first move); capture diagonally.
  • Rooks: Move horizontally or vertically any number of squares.
  • Knights: Move in an "L" shape (2 squares one way, 1 square to the side). Knights can jump over other pieces.
  • Bishops: Move diagonally any number of squares.
  • Queen: Moves horizontally, vertically, or diagonally any number of squares.
  • King: Moves 1 square in any direction. Protect him at all costs!
