♟️ How to Play Master Chess (Official Rules)

🎯 Objective

Checkmate your opponent's King. Checkmate occurs when the King is under attack (in check) and has no legal move to escape capture.

♟️ Pawn Rules

Pawns move forward 1 square. On their very first move, they can move 2 squares forward. Pawns capture diagonally forward 1 square. If a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board, it promotes to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

♜ Rook & ♝ Bishop

Rooks move horizontally or vertically any number of squares.
Bishops move diagonally any number of open squares.

♞ Knight (The Jumper)

Knights move in an "L" shape (2 squares in one direction, 1 square perpendicular). The Knight is the only piece that can jump over other pieces!

♛ Queen & ♚ King

Queen: The most powerful piece. Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
King: Moves 1 square in any direction. Never let your King get captured!

⚔️ Special Moves

Castling: Move your King 2 squares towards a Rook, and the Rook hops next to the King (if neither piece has moved yet and no pieces are in between).
En Passant: Capture an opposing pawn that just moved 2 squares forward past yours.
